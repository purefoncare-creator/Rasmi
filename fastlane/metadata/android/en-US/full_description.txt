Purefon is a free and open-source phone app for Android that combines a powerful dialer, SMS, and MMS messaging into one clean, fast experience.

Features:
• Dialer with call history, favorites, and contact search
• SMS and MMS messaging with image, video, and audio support
• Dual SIM support
• Scheduled messages
• Conversation templates
• Message scheduling and reminders
• Call screening and blocking
• Spam detection
• Contact management with avatars
• Location sharing
• Audio messages
• Dark mode
• English and Arabic languages

Purefon uses Android's built-in location services and does not require Google Play Services.
